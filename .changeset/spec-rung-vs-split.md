The `spec` skill now names the two relationships between specs: rung (a design spec above a functional spec) and split (parent and child design specs at one rung). Split by capability says a child is a design spec and never the functional spec, and a functional spec's use cases are described as related to, not mapped one to one with, the design spec's. The glossary gains rung, split, and functional spec.
